Our Story

Partners First.
Everything Else Second.

Nexos Digital Solutions was founded by Andres Silva and Dylan Whitehead — two former college tennis teammates who turned a shared frustration into a mission.

Local businesses kept getting sold tools they didn't need by agencies that never took the time to understand their problems. We built Nexos to change that — to make powerful automation, AI, and outreach tools accessible to the businesses that need them most.

We're hungry. And we deliver.
